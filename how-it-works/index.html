How it works

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

Cozine helps Discord moderators identify Roblox exploiters and cheaters by checking users against documented records and monitored sources. It is designed to support moderation decisions.

1. Add Cozine to your server

Invite Cozine to your Discord server (or add it as an app) and grant the permissions required for the features you want to use. For join alerts and trace/webhook-based notifications, webhook-related permissions may be required. See the homepage setup steps for details.

2. Scan users on demand

Moderators and authorized users can scan Discord users with commands like /skid (and available context actions). Cozine looks up linked Roblox information and checks against its records to report whether a user is flagged and what relevant details are available based on access level and settings.

3. Monitor joins and receive alerts

When your server is configured, Cozine can check new members as they join. If a flagged user is detected, Cozine can notify your moderation team in the configured channel so staff can review the evidence and decide what action to take.

4. Alert-only or automated actions

Servers can choose how to respond: keep alerts for moderator review, or enable supported automated actions (such as kicking flagged users) where configured. Final moderation decisions and outcomes are the responsibility of server moderators, as described in our Terms of Service.

5. Manage settings in the panel

The Cozine Panel lets eligible users sign in with Discord to view plan/cooldown information, scan history, and manage preferences (such as /skid-related settings and privacy options for command error logging), subject to access and beta availability.

Accuracy, appeals, and privacy

  • Flags are based on documented evidence, but mistakes can happen. Users can appeal through the support server or the panel.
  • Use Cozine for legitimate moderation and safety only. Do not harass, target, or dox users.
  • For data collection, retention, and deletion details, read our Privacy Policy.
